Government Structure

Who Actually Governs Pelham?

Pelham is unique: the entire Town is made up of two incorporated Villages. That means Village governments handle most day-to-day services, while Town government handles shared functions. Then add the School District, County, and State on top.

How it's structured

Five bodies levy taxes or make decisions here. Two cover everyone, two cover only their own village, and the school district cuts across both.

All Pelham residentsVillage residents only
Westchester County Every Pelham resident

Health department, parks, sewer districts, social services, county police — and the equalization rates used in property assessments.

Town of Pelham Every Pelham resident

Municipal courts, tax assessment and collection, recreation programmes, the public library, and the ambulance service shared by both villages.

Village of Pelham Village residents only

Police, fire, refuse, water, roads and the building department — for Village of Pelham residents only.

Village of Pelham Manor Village residents only

Its own police, fire and public works departments — for Pelham Manor residents only.

Pelham Union Free School District Every Pelham resident

K–12 education across four elementary schools, the middle school and Pelham Memorial High School.

What each body controls

Village Government

Village of Pelham

The primary local government for residents of "Pelham Heights" and former North Pelham. The Board of Trustees sets policy, manages the budget, and runs most everyday services — police, fire, garbage, water, and roads.

Town Government

Town of Pelham

The Town umbrella covers both villages. Rather than duplicating services, it handles shared functions that make sense to consolidate: municipal courts, tax assessment & collection, recreation programs, the public library, and ambulance service.

School District

Pelham Union Free School District

An elected Board of Education governs K–12 education across four elementary schools, one middle school, and Pelham Memorial High School. The school district has its own budget voted on separately by residents each May.

County Government

Westchester County

County government provides regional services including the county police, health department, parks, sewer districts, and social services. Westchester is also the entity that establishes equalization rates used in property tax assessments.

Quick Reference

Who to call for what?

Pothole on your street? → Village DPW

Noise complaint? → Village Police

Tax bill question? → Town Receiver of Taxes

School issue? → Board of Education

Parks & rec programs? → Town Recreation Dept

Property assessment dispute? → Town Assessor

Library hours? → Pelham Public Library (Town-run)
