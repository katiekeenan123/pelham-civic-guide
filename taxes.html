Follow the Money

Where Do Your Property Taxes Go?

The school district is the largest share of a Pelham property tax bill — ~68% for a Village of Pelham homestead. The village takes ~27% (~26% in Pelham Manor), the county ~3–4% and the town ~1–2%.

🏫 School District
FY2026-27 PUFSD budget: $98.3M, voter-approved May 2026. Tax levy rose 3.48% — at state cap. Voters approved Props 1 & 2 of $143.6M capital bond in May 2025.
School budget documents
🏛️ Westchester County
County funds regional services — health, parks, social services. Your 14th District legislator advocates for Pelham's share.
County budget
🏘️ Village of Pelham
Village taxes fund police, fire, garbage, roads, water and the building department. The Village of Pelham passed a $20.5M budget (10.1% increase) for FY2026-27, overriding the state tax cap.
Village adopted budgets
🏘️ Village of Pelham Manor
Pelham Manor runs its own police, fire and public works. Its FY2026-27 budget is $21.9M with a 2.85% tax increase, under the state cap.
Manor budgets & financial statements
🏡 Town of Pelham
Smallest share — funds the services it would be wasteful to duplicate in each village: EMS (ambulance) for every Pelham resident, tax assessment and collection, and the town courts, plus the library and recreation.
