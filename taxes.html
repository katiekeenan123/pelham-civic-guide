Where Do Your Property Taxes Go?

Village taxes represent approximately 25% of your total property tax bill (per Mayor Mullen, Pelham Examiner May 2026). The school district is the largest share.

Note: Village ~25% confirmed (Mayor Mullen/Examiner). School district is the largest share — no published %. County % unverified. On an equivalent assessed value of $1,045,204, a Village of Pelham homeowner pays ~$6,807 against ~$6,035 in Pelham Manor; the median Village of Pelham bill for FY2026-27 is ~$7,387. Call Town Receiver of Taxes (914-738-1642) for your specific breakdown.
🏫 School District
FY2026-27 PUFSD budget: $98.3M, voter-approved May 2026. Tax levy rose 3.48% — at state cap. Voters approved Props 1 & 2 of $143.6M capital bond in May 2025.

🏘️ Village Services
Village taxes fund police, fire, garbage, roads, water, and building dept. Village of Pelham passed $20.5M budget (10.1% increase) for FY2026-27, overriding the state tax cap.
