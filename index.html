Your guide to Pelham civic life.

Our mission is to increase civic awareness and engagement in local matters in Pelham, NY — by making public information accessible, understandable, and actionable for every resident.

Pelham has layered government across a Town, two Villages, a School District, and County. This guide cuts through the complexity so you can show up informed, ask better questions, and make your voice count.
