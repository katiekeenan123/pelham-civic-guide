Rising Property Taxes & Village Debt

Village of Pelham adopted $20.5M FY2026-27 budget — 10.1% increase overriding the state tax cap. Village debt grew from $4.26M in 2021 to $17.88M by early 2026. The fiscal debate is central to November 2026 election.

Personnel

Receiver of Taxes Vacancy — Appointment or Referendum Under Discussion

Erica Winter, elected Town Receiver of Taxes in November 2025, stepped down after less than a year citing a new professional opportunity. Deputy Receiver Darlene Paolericio assumed her duties and continues to fulfill them. The Town Board made no appointment at its September 14, 2026 meeting and is weighing whether to appoint a replacement or hold a public referendum to make the position permanently appointed rather than elected; full discussion is scheduled for the October 2026 meeting. The Receiver of Taxes is responsible for collecting property tax payments from all Pelham residents.

Active — replacement under discussion — board weighing appointment vs. referendum; October meeting scheduled

Education

Colonial Elementary — AC Interim Fix in Place

After parents Ashley Mills and incoming PTA President Concetta Eudicone spoke at the June 24 Board of Ed meeting demanding action, the Colonial, Siwanoy, and Prospect Hill PTAs pledged to raise funds for window AC units. New units were installed in time for the first day of school on September 8, 2026. Full HVAC conversion for all schools is still coming Summer 2027 as part of the $56.2M capital bond approved in May 2025.

Interim fix in place — full HVAC conversion Summer 2027

Energy

Con Edison Rate Case — Settlement Cuts the Increase

Con Edison filed for a large rate increase and the Village of Pelham joined a consortium of 40 Westchester municipalities opposing it. A settlement has since cut the electric increase to about 2.8% of the total customer bill, down from the 13.4% originally proposed, and the gas increase to about 2%. The agreement is not final: the Public Service Commission holds an evidentiary hearing before issuing a decision.

Settlement reached — awaiting Public Service Commission approval
Pelham Examiner
Public Safety

Fini Park — Nightly Illegal Fireworks

Residents reported nightly illegal fireworks from Fini Park through summer 2026, often past 11pm. At the July 14 2026 Village board meeting a resident described calling New Rochelle police up to six times a night against only 12 calls logged in their records since Memorial Day. Trustee Howell committed to increased Pelham PD patrols on the Harman/Store Avenue corridor between 8 and 11pm. Residents can send videos to krystal.howell@pelhamny.gov.

Ongoing — increased PPD patrols committed
