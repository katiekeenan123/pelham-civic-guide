Government Structure

Who Actually Governs Pelham?

Pelham is unusual: the entire Town is made up of two incorporated villages. The villages run most day-to-day services; the Town runs the ones it would be wasteful to duplicate in each village, like EMS, tax collection and the courts; and the school district runs the schools for everyone. Westchester County is different in kind — for most residents it is a share of the property tax bill and a set of county-wide services such as county police, the courts, health and parks, rather than a local board you deal with directly.

How it's structured

Five bodies levy taxes or make decisions here. Two cover everyone, two cover only their own village, and the school district cuts across both.
