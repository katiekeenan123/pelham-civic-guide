📅 August 17, 2026 ⏱ 37 min 🏘️ Village of Pelham Manor 👥 Deputy Mayor Bennett (chair) · Trustee Owen-Michaane absent ▶ Watch recording

A brisk regular monthly meeting — departmental reports, two public hearings, and several routine approvals, all carried unanimously. Deputy Mayor Breda Bennett presided; Trustee Maurice Owen-Michaane was absent, travelling out of state. The board opened with condolences for two long-time residents, Frank Barbieri, a roughly 30-year volunteer firefighter who reached lieutenant, and Donna DelPonte.

The Amtrak project on Forest Road drew the evening's only public comment. Resident and volunteer firefighter Josh Casper reported that Amtrak had leafleted only a handful of households, that some work will run 24/7 with track shutdowns, and that Amtrak's representative could not say how noise would be measured or reported. He also flagged litter left by past crews and a rail gate left open during work — a safety risk with children nearby. The board confirmed the corridor project is expected to run three years, said the contact on Amtrak's flyers has been responsive, and directed two immediate steps: extra police patrol sweeps, and deployment of the village's mobile spotlight along the street to raise visible presence without shining it at homes.

Finances are strong. Total fund balance at the end of July was roughly $19 million, with about $15 million in real property taxes running in line with budget. A FY2025–26 line-item budget amendment — a Comptroller-recommended cleanup before audit — passed at net zero cost to taxpayers.
