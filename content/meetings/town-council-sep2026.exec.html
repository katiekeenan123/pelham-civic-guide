📅 September 14, 2026 ⏱ 82 min 👥 Supervisor Mohan (chair) · Town Council ▶ Watch recording

The Town Council met on September 14, 2026, chaired by Town Supervisor Theresa Mohan. The board took the agenda out of order to hear the independent auditor first, then worked through EMS, library, recreation and budget reports and remarks from State Senator Fernandez. Two significant items were deliberately left open: the vacant Receiver of Taxes seat, and the discovery that a town board has been paid for years without a resolution authorizing it. Both were pushed to October.

The audit was the headline. The town's outside auditor issued an unmodified — "clean" — opinion for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2025, the highest level of assurance available, and found no material weakness or significant deficiency. The town finished roughly $860,000 ahead of its overall budget; general fund balance grew from about $2.2M to about $2.45M, with unassigned fund balance near 20% of the 2026 adopted budget. Revenues beat the final budget by about $1.17M, led by ambulance revenue, a state ambulance grant, parks and recreation charges and investment income — but ambulance expenditures ran $391,000 over budget, and the auditor told the board to carry ambulance insurance, pension and OPEB costs into 2027 budget planning.

No appointment was made to the vacant Receiver of Taxes seat. Deputy Receiver Darlene Paolericio continues to carry all the duties, which Town Law assigns to her automatically until someone is appointed or elected. Supervisor Mohan put a second option on the table: a public referendum that would end the elected office and make the position appointed by the board — the job would still exist, but voters would no longer fill it. Because the vacancy falls within three months of the November election, it cannot appear on this year's ballot; an appointee would serve to the end of next year, an elected successor would start January 2028, and a referendum could go to voters next year. Full discussion is scheduled for the October meeting.
