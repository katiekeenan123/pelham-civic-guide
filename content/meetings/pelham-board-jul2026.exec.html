The Board held its regular July meeting chaired by Deputy Mayor Carpenter (Mayor Mullen absent). The session covered infrastructure, public safety, and quality-of-life concerns. Two residents raised urgent complaints about Hudson House construction disruption and nightly illegal fireworks near Fini Park — both prompted board action commitments. All formal votes passed unanimously 6-0.

Key highlights: final delivery of NYSERDA grant items (two electric refuse haulers completing a $200,000+ fleet electrification); preliminary approval from Westchester County for $24.25 million in stormwater funding; and a mayor's plan to sign a letter supporting county-wide sewer consolidation.
