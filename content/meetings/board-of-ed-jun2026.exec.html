📅 June 24, 2026 ⏱ 84 min 🏫 Pelham Union Free School District 👥 VP Natalie Marrero (chair) · President De Angelis absent ▶ Watch recording

The final Business Meeting of the 2025–26 school year. Board President Jackie De Angelis was absent and Vice President Natalie Marrero presided at her request. The board reviewed a full year against its Strategic Plan, recognized student and staff achievements, and cleared significant end-of-year financial and policy business. Every vote was unanimous.

Classroom heat at Colonial Elementary dominated public comment. Three parents — including incoming PTA President Concetta Eudicone — pressed for an interim cooling solution before September. Superintendent Dr. Champ confirmed full central air is coming Summer 2027, not 2028–29 as some had heard, and explained the interim constraint: many classroom circuits cannot carry an AC unit large enough to cool the room. Parents have offered to donate or fundraise for units, but the district must finish an electrical capacity assessment first. Colonial's middle-level rooms were approaching the state's 88°F threshold, and school was dismissed early the previous Friday. Update: The PTAs followed through — window AC units were installed at Colonial, Siwanoy, and Prospect Hill in time for the first day of school on September 8, 2026. Full HVAC conversion remains on track for Summer 2027.

On finances, a $30.5M Bond Anticipation Note sold at 2.644% — well under the 3.25% budgeted estimate. The budget passed with 75% voter approval, up 14 points year over year and within the tax cap, and the audit found no material weaknesses or significant deficiencies.
