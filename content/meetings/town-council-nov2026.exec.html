📅 November 9, 2026 ⏱ 70 min 👥 Supervisor Mohan (chair) · Town Council ▶ Watch recording

A legislative meeting chaired by Town Supervisor Theresa Mohan, with the full board present. After public comment and reports, the board changed the Town's insurance broker and coverage, introduced a local law that would let the Town exceed the state tax cap for 2027, set the December budget calendar, and voted to begin employee contributions to health insurance premiums. It also approved an Eagle Scout project, an EMS Advisory Committee appointment, budget transfers and the October 5 minutes, then went into executive session on personnel salary adjustments.

The Town's insurance moves to NYMIR. The board appointed Brown & Brown as the Town's insurance broker and directed placement of coverage with the New York Municipal Insurance Reciprocal (NYMIR) and PERMA [VERIFY] for 2026–27. The current coverage, procured through the local agency Meridian and written by Travelers, expires at the end of November, and the board was told it faced an increase of as much as 16%. Next year's commercial liability insurance had been estimated at about $325,000–$350,000 in the tentative budget; board members called the switch a substantial saving they expect to continue, though the NYMIR premium itself was not read out. According to the presenters, NYMIR's coverage roughly matches the current $12 million per-occurrence liability limit, about doubles the aggregate, and adds cyber coverage the Town does not now have. In public comment, the head of Meridian, Joe Salimini [VERIFY], said his agency has handled the Town's insurance for about ten years, urged the board to choose "the best program and the best value" rather than act out of loyalty to a local business, and offered to help with a smooth transition. Supervisor Mohan called it "one of the biggest decisions we've made as a board."

Tax cap: the tentative budget is about $97,000 over. The Supervisor said the levy in the tentative 2027 budget would put the Town about $97,000 above the state tax cap, and that "in an abundance of caution" the Town should preserve its right to exceed it. The board introduced a proposed local law (Local Law No. 2) to override the levy limit for fiscal 2027 and scheduled a public hearing on it for December 7. No decision to exceed the cap was made.

Budget calendar. The tentative budget was filed on October 30 with the Supervisor's Budget Guide No. 2. The board will meet again within two weeks to adopt a preliminary budget; the public hearing on the preliminary budget follows the Local Law No. 2 hearing on December 7, and the budget vote is set for December 14.

Employee health insurance. The Town has historically paid 100% of health insurance premiums for full-time employees. The resolution sets a contribution rate for new employees and a three-year phase-in for current ones; it goes next to the New York State Health Insurance Program and can take effect in about six months. One board member argued against it, estimating next year's premiums at about $20,000 (individual) and $44,000 (family) and the eventual cost to employees at $3,000–$8,000 a year, "basically a pay cut", against a saving to Pelham households of about $15 to $36 a year if spread across all employees; the member said, "I don't see health care as a benefit, I see it as a right." Supervisor Mohan said the change is part of a broader package to make Town salaries competitive, including a raise to offset the contributions on top of a 3% raise. The resolution carried; the individual votes are not clear on the recording, so check the Town's minutes for the tally.

Also: EMS answered 129 calls in the past month against a usual 100 or so, with an average response time of 5.24 minutes. The EMS Advisory Committee holds its first community meeting on Monday, November 16 at 6 p.m.; the location will be posted on the Town website. Councilman Jenks said cost estimates for the library transformation project are expected as the design phase ends. The Supervisor thanked Deputy Town Clerk Molly Girolamo for her handling of Election Day.
