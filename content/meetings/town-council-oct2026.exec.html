📅 October 5, 2026 ⏱ 36 min 👥 Supervisor Mohan (chair) · Town Council ▶ Watch recording

A short legislative meeting chaired by Town Supervisor Theresa Mohan, with the full board present. Public comment came first and was dominated by the EMS station site; the board then heard EMS, Glover Field, library and recreation reports, the Supervisor's report, and passed four resolutions and the September 14 minutes, all unanimously.

No decision has been made on where a new EMS station goes. Residents near the post office came to oppose the property next to it, which they had understood was off the table; one called it "just a bad location" for an ambulance even if it were free, citing stop signs, residential streets and an already hard-to-cross road. The board said the two options are still the current station, whose parking lot floods in rain, or that property, if it can be had for $50,000 and is feasible to build on. It is not an immediate priority: the Master Facilities Plan's issues at the Daronco Town House come first. A grant for the station was not awarded, but the Town can reapply, and the board promised to involve neighbors before any decision. It also rejected what it called misinformation that it had approved a multi-million-dollar station: the estimate (first given as $3.6 million, then corrected on the record to $2.6 million) was drafted in May 2024 by a consultant working from a previous board's information, and neither board approved it.

EMS contract talks start October 7. The Supervisor said negotiations with EVAC on its contract, which expires at the end of the year, begin the day after the meeting, and the board appointed the new EMS Advisory Committee. In September the service answered 108 calls with an average response time of about 5.5 to 5.7 minutes across Pelham and Pelham Manor; for cardiac arrests, typically under four to five minutes.

Budget season: a candidate for Town Supervisor asked for a budget overview before Election Day, since the November meeting falls after it. The board pointed to an open budget session on October 14. The recording gives two filing dates: the budget "has to be filed with the town clerk on October 10th", and later the tentative budget is due October 30, the Town being on a schedule with "an extra month". A final budget vote is expected in December. The November 2 meeting moves to November 9 at 7 p.m., because voting machines arrive at the Town House the evening before Election Day and the Town Clerk runs the election.
