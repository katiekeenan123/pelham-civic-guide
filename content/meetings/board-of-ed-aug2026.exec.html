📅 August 26, 2026 ⏱ 73 min 🏫 Pelham Union Free School District 👥 Board President De Angelis (chair) · Dr. Champ ▶ Watch recording

The last business meeting before students returned, given over almost entirely to setting direction for 2026–27: board goals, governance protocols, an organizational chart, committee roles, a new board charter, staffing, and advocacy. No members of the public attended, so the public comment period was skipped. Every vote was unanimous.

Air conditioning was ready for day one. This is the direct sequel to the June 24 meeting, where Colonial parents asked for an interim fix. Window and portable units are in at all three elementary schools — 8 at Colonial, 4 each at Prospect Hill and Siwanoy — covering classroom spaces and the Colonial nurse's office on the main floor. The district bought the units up front to get consistent models, BTU ratings and pricing; the PTA Council approved the fundraising process on August 25 and the PTAs will reimburse. Dr. Champ said the aim is "so that we don't have to close school for heat again," and credited the parents who "stepped forward in June." Once central air arrives in 2027, the window units move to the high school, which was left out of the capital projects — "not the most elegant solution, but it's functional."

Pelham's U.S. News ranking fell for a reason outside the district. The State Education Department did not authorize release of AP participation and performance data to U.S. News, hitting high-performing districts statewide. Board President De Angelis said the drop "did not happen because our AP participation or our performance declined in any way," and that this year's ranking "does not provide an accurate comparative picture."

The board also accepted a three-year JED Foundation partnership at no district cost — roughly $30,000/year, about $90,000 in value, funded by an AASA scholarship — replacing the concluded BOCES recovery grant. Screen time and EdTech guidelines drafted over the summer come to the board on October 7. After a brief executive session the board voted to deny a student transportation appeal.
