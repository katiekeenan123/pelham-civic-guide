📅 September 14, 2026 ⏱ 17 min 🏘️ Village of Pelham Manor 👥 Mayor Lapey (chair) · Deputy Mayor Bennett and Trustee Winstead absent ▶ Watch recording

A short regular meeting chaired by Mayor Lapey, with Deputy Mayor Bennett and Trustee Winstead both away on family matters — leaving a quorum but only three members present. The agenda was routine and moved quickly: departmental reports, four community event approvals, a voucher resolution, and an adjournment into executive session. No member of the public spoke.

The evening's weight was in the remembrances. The board marked the 25th anniversary of September 11 and recognised Paul Satorius, a Pelham Manor Fire Department member whose death has been officially classified as 9/11-related. His name was read at the American Legion Post 50 and Town of Pelham ceremony at 9/11 Memorial Park, added to the Rising Memorial wall, and will appear at the Westchester County Firefighters Memorial in October. The Mayor also announced the deaths of two long-time residents, Tish Davie and Kay Teeley.

Stormwater work was credited with keeping storm damage down. Through several large rainstorms and flash floods in the preceding weeks, storm-related emergency call volume was described as "almost nil" — the Fire Commissioner logged four minor water conditions, none of them storm-related — while at least one neighbouring community saw flooding serious enough to require a vehicle rescue. The next project, on Monroe Street, goes out to bid by the end of September. Separately, the Fire Department flagged 10 motor vehicle accidents on I-95 in August, which State Police are investigating as suspected insurance fraud: drivers cutting in front of trucks and braking hard to cause rear-end collisions.
