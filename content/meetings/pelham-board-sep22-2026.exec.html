📅 September 22, 2026 ⏱ 33 min 👥 Mayor Mullen (chair) · five of six Trustees present ▶ Watch recording

A short, businesslike regular meeting chaired by Mayor Chance Mullen. Five of the six trustees were present — Trustee Eldahry [VERIFY] was absent — so every recorded vote was 6–0. No members of the public attended, and the board finished its agenda in just over half an hour.

The DPW's switch to electric landscaping equipment is paying off. Trustee Anderson reported on a walk of Wolfs Lane Park with the Mayor, DPW staff and Dan Maib [VERIFY] of the American Green Zone Alliance. Staff who started out "iffy" about the equipment now call it "the best thing we've ever done", and oil use has fallen from roughly a case every other week to about a case a year. The whole Wolfs Lane Park system may qualify for Level 1 Green Zone certification, which makes electric equipment the standard for routine maintenance while still allowing gas for seasonal and storm cleanup. A certification pledge will come to the board.

A front loader the board had hesitated over was approved. The Avant 640i front loader for DPW snow removal was not budgeted, and at the previous work session Deputy Mayor Carpenter objected to taking so much from contingency this early in the fiscal year. The Deputy Mayor said a conversation with Administrator Scelza about revenue expected this fiscal year — not yet public — settled that concern. The Mayor added that selling the surplus aerial ladder truck could also replenish the fund.

Also approved: a modified site plan for 64 Lincoln Avenue and 216 Third Avenue, conditional on a stormwater drainage plan before any building permit; the adjusted base proportions for the 2027 village tax levy, using the 2025 final assessment roll; and Greenberg Traurig LLP as Village Attorney and eminent domain counsel, after the village attorney moved to that firm at unchanged rates. Trustee Solomon said talks with stakeholders on school zone speed cameras are only just beginning, and the Mayor announced "Coffees with the Mayor" every other Friday morning through December.
