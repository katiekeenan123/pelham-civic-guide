📅 August 3, 2026 ⏱ 30 min 👥 Supervisor Mohan (chair) · Town Council ▶ Watch recording

The Town Council held its regular monthly meeting on August 3, 2026, chaired by Town Supervisor Theresa Mohan. The session was largely routine — departmental updates, two housekeeping resolutions, and an acknowledgment of a longtime community volunteer. All formal votes passed unanimously.

Accessibility at Town Hall was the through-line. One resident pressed the board on acoustics and the absence of an elevator; the board confirmed an acoustical engineer will be engaged and said an elevator is "not off the table" but that the originally proposed location would have cost over $600,000. The working plan is to move all public-facing services to the first floor instead — a shift already underway, with the Town Clerk's office relocated and a phone installed at the entrance so residents who cannot climb stairs can call for assistance.
